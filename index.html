Ads Cockpit

Ads Cockpit is a private, locally run tool used by a single freelance advertising manager to analyse the Google Ads, Google Analytics and Google Merchant Center accounts of clients they manage.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it is not offered to other users. It runs only on the developer's own computer.
