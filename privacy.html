Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What Ads Cockpit is

Ads Cockpit is a private tool that runs only on the developer's own computer. It is used by one person to manage advertising for clients who have given that person access to their accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

When the developer signs in with their Google account, Ads Cockpit requests read access to Google Ads, Google Analytics and Google Merchant Center data of accounts that the developer already has access to. Changes to Google Ads are only made after the developer explicitly approves each change.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to analyse advertising performance and prepare recommendations for the accounts it was retrieved from.
  • Data is stored only locally on the developer's computer. It is not uploaded to any server operated by Ads Cockpit.
  • For analysis and explanations, the developer may use an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic, on the developer's own account). Only the data needed for that analysis is sent, and it is processed by Anthropic under its own terms.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling.
  • Data from Google user data APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine learning models.

Ads Cockpit's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Local data is kept only as long as it is needed for the client relationship and is deleted from the developer's computer when no longer needed. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer via github.com/emilehuijbregts.
